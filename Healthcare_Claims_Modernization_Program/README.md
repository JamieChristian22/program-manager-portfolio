## 📌 Program Overview

This program simulates a healthcare organization modernizing its claims processing systems to improve:

- Operational efficiency  
- Compliance and risk controls  
- Member and provider experience  
- Reporting visibility  
- Executive oversight  

It reflects environments such as payers, healthcare IT vendors, consulting firms, and health systems.
