## 📌 Program Overview

The **Customer Experience Optimization Program** is a simulated 10-month, cross-functional enterprise transformation designed to improve the end-to-end customer experience across digital self-service, contact-center operations, CRM workflows, knowledge management, and Voice of Customer capabilities.

The program coordinates **five interconnected workstreams** under a single governance structure to improve customer satisfaction while simultaneously reducing service friction, repeat contacts, resolution time, and operating costs.

Rather than treating customer experience improvement as a collection of independent projects, this program demonstrates how a Program Manager aligns **strategy, people, processes, technology, data, dependencies, financial resources, KPIs, and organizational change** around shared customer and business outcomes.

> **Portfolio Disclosure:** This is a realistic professional program-management simulation. Organizations, budgets, stakeholders, financial benefits, operational metrics, and performance results are fictionalized for portfolio demonstration and are not presented as employment history.

# 🎯 Business Challenge

Customer interactions were fragmented across digital and assisted-service channels.

The simulated current-state environment contained several common enterprise CX challenges:

* Inconsistent customer experiences across channels
* High repeat-contact rates
* Long resolution times
* Limited digital self-service
* Fragmented Voice of Customer data
* Inconsistent knowledge content
* Manual customer-service workflows
* Limited executive visibility into CX performance
* Different KPI definitions across teams
* CRM configuration constraints
* Agent adoption challenges
* Limited ownership of customer-experience benefits

The organization required more than isolated service improvements.

It needed an integrated program capable of coordinating **customer insights, technology improvements, operational redesign, employee adoption, governance, and measurable business benefits**.

---

# 🎯 Program Objectives

The program was structured around five strategic objectives.

### 1. Improve Customer Satisfaction

Increase Net Promoter Score and Customer Satisfaction through more consistent and effective customer journeys.

### 2. Reduce Customer Effort

Remove unnecessary handoffs, repeated information requests, dead-end digital journeys, and avoidable escalations.

### 3. Improve Service Performance

Reduce resolution time and repeat contacts while increasing first-contact resolution.

### 4. Expand Digital Self-Service

Enable customers to successfully complete more common service activities without agent intervention while preserving clear human escalation paths.

### 5. Establish Sustainable CX Governance

Create a repeatable Voice of Customer, KPI, benefits, and executive-governance model that continues after program closure.

# 🔗 Integrated Program Model

The workstreams were deliberately managed as an interconnected program.

```text
Voice of Customer
        ↓

        ↓
Journey Prioritization
        ↓
Digital Self-Service ─────────────┐
                                  │
Contact Center Process ───────────┼──→ Improved Customer Experience
                                  │
CRM & Knowledge ──────────────────┘
        ↓
Change + Training
        ↓
Employee Adoption
        ↓
KPI Measurement
        ↓
Benefits Realization
```

A change in one workstream could affect several others.

For example:

**VOC Insight → Journey Redesign → CRM Configuration → Knowledge Update → Agent Training → Production Release → KPI Measurement**

This dependency model demonstrates why the initiative requires **program-level coordination rather than isolated project management**.

# 🧠 Lessons Learned

### 1. Dependencies Need Acceptance Criteria

A date alone does not define a successful handoff.

Dependencies should identify exactly what the downstream team needs to accept.

### 2. KPI Definitions Must Be Governed Early

Customer metrics can create disagreement when teams use different formulas or data sources.

A KPI charter should define:

* Formula
* Data source
* Owner
* Frequency
* Baseline
* Target

### 3. Change Management Is Part of Delivery

Agent adoption cannot be treated as a post-launch activity.

Training, communications, leadership reinforcement, and adoption measurement must be integrated into the program plan.

### 4. Customer Experience Requires Cross-Functional Ownership

Customer pain points frequently cross organizational boundaries.

Program governance provides the mechanism for coordinating those boundaries.

### 5. Benefits Ownership Must Continue After Closure

The Program Manager establishes the benefits framework, but sustainable realization ultimately belongs to business and operational owners.
