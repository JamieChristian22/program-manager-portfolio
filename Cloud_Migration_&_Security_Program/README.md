# ⚠️ RAID Management

Program-level RAID management covers:

### Risks

Potential future events that could affect strategic outcomes.

### Assumptions

Planning conditions requiring validation throughout delivery.

### Issues

Current problems requiring resolution and accountable ownership.

### Dependencies

Cross-workstream relationships capable of affecting the critical path.

Risks and issues are evaluated through:

**Probability × Impact × Business Exposure × Response Strategy**
